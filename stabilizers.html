Tool 05 · Stabilizer Circuit Builder

Build a stabilizer code, then break it

Pick a code, inject Pauli errors on physical qubits, and watch which stabilizers fire. This is the syndrome extraction step every decoder in the suite consumes — made explicit, generator by generator.

01 Code & qubits

Click a physical qubit to cycle through no error → X → Z → Y → no error. Stabilizers whose support overlaps an anticommuting error will fire.

02 Syndrome

Inject an error, then press Decode to see the correction the syndrome implies.

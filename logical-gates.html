Applying gates to encoded qubits

The Steane [[7,1,3]] code, one logical qubit at a time. Clifford gates (H, S, CNOT) act transversally — one physical gate per physical qubit, no interaction between blocks required for fault tolerance. T does not, by the Eastin–Knill theorem, so it needs a magic state instead.
